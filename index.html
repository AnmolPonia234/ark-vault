What Ark Vault does

Many of your important things live inside online accounts: photos, documents, recipes, customer records. If you die, lose access, or a company shuts down, your family may not even know those accounts exist. Ark Vault helps you list them, record where your copies are, and lock that list so only a group of people you trust can open it together.

How to use it

  1. Connect. Open the list of apps you signed in to with Google, Apple or Facebook, copy it, and paste it into Ark. Ark adds the accounts it recognises and asks you about the rest.
  2. Scan files (optional). Add accounts you did not sign in to with those services by uploading a mail export or a browser password export.
  3. Inventory. For each account, mark how important it is, what is at stake, and where your saved copy lives. Aim for a copy of every Irreplaceable account. The Save and convert tab turns exports into open formats and counts down shutdown dates.
  4. Lock and share. Name 2 to 8 trusted people and choose how many must cooperate, for example 3 of 5. Ark produces one encrypted vault text and one piece for each person.
  5. Hand over. Send each person their piece by a different route. Keep the vault text somewhere safe, such as your cloud drive. Later, those people paste the vault text and enough pieces into step 5 to open it and read your guide.
If you are a family member helping after a loss, go straight to step 5. You need the vault text and pieces from enough of the trusted people.

Good to know

  • Already have an export? Drop the ZIP in the Drop export tab. Ark unpacks and checks it on your device and marks that account as saved. You can then save an encrypted second copy to another drive.
  • Privacy. Files are processed in this browser and are not uploaded by the app. The page loads JSZip from a third-party CDN. Inventory details are stored in this browser’s local storage without encryption until you create a locked vault. Do not use this prototype for real secrets.
  • Your list stays in this browser. Inventory data is saved in local storage on this device in readable form until you create a locked vault. Clear browser data or a different browser profile may make it unavailable.
  • One piece alone is useless. That is the safety feature. Do not keep all pieces in one place.
  • Test it once. Try opening the vault with your own pieces before you rely on it, then review every six months.
  • Limits. Ark does not bypass security or log in for anyone, does not send automatic alerts, and is not legal advice. Follow each provider’s official process for accounts after a death.

Import from Google, Apple and Facebook

Each service keeps a list of the apps and sites you signed in to with it. Google, Apple and Meta do not let other apps read that list, so Ark cannot sign in and fetch it for you. Instead you open your own list, copy it, and paste it here. The text stays in this tab.

Lock your inventory

Ark encrypts your inventory with a random passphrase, then splits that passphrase into threshold shares. The number of shares required is printed on every share. This prototype has not been independently security-audited.

Where everything is

Drop an export

Download your data from Google (Takeout), Apple (privacy.apple.com) or Meta (Download your information), then drop the ZIP here. Ark opens it on your device, shows what is inside, turns notes and contacts into open formats, and marks that account as saved in your Inventory.

Drop ZIP files here
or tap to choose files
After you drop a ZIP, press Save encrypted second copy. Ark encrypts it with a random passphrase and offers the copy as a file to save. Keep that file on a different device or drive than the original. The passphrase is shown once after encryption. Save it separately from the encrypted file; Ark does not retain it. Large exports of several gigabytes may not open in a browser, so ask the service to split the export into smaller parts.

Restore a second copy

Choose an Ark copy file and its passphrase. Ark decrypts it here and offers your original ZIP back.

Converted from your export

Shutdown watcher

Ark cannot watch the news for you. When you hear that a service is closing, add it here and Ark counts down the days and tells you what to do. Automatic monitoring would need a server.

Convert an export to an open format

Paste or upload an export (JSON list or CSV with a header row). Ark converts it in this tab. Copy the result and save it as a file on your computer: .md, .vcf or .csv.
